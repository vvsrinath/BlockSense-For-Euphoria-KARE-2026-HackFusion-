# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in BlockSense to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds a welcoming community:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people who are learning.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Being honest about mistakes, and taking responsibility for them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it.
- Showing empathy towards other contributors.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions — and when an individual is officially representing the project in
public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers by opening a confidential
report through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting, or by contacting the
maintainer directly.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The
maintainer is obliged to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any
incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the
project's leadership.
